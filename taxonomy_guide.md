# Motivation & Context
This typology is made to be able to analyze what posts are about on the r/McGill and r/Concordia subreddits.

# Overview of the types

## Course question/advice
Any post about a specific course. 

Positive examples:
- Should I take COMP 370 with my COMP minor?
- MATH 323 Midterm

Negative examples:
- You can get free pizza if you fail a course
- What type of courses do you have to take in a Tuba major?

Edge cases:
- How do I submit my assignment in crowdmark for COMP 370?
    - Included: Even though it's talking about logistics, its in the context of one specific course.
- How do I get on the waitlist for COMP 302?
    - Excluded: Even though it's talking about a specific course, it's about the logistics *outside* the course.

## Program question/advice
Any post about a program or about choosing a program.

Positive examples:
- Should I switch to MATH minor?
- What do yall think about a double major COMP & BIO?

Negative examples:
- CALLING ALL COMP MAJORS: Come get free icecream if you failed a programming assigment!
- Who's tryna join my D&D campaign?

Edge cases:
- Can I substitute this CS complementary course to count toward my degree requirements?
    - Included: The post asks whether a course substitution changes program requirements, degree progress, or program fit.
- What courses should I choose for my math minor?
    - Included: Even though the post talks about courses, it's not talking about any specific ones and it's more
    asking about advice for the math minor. 

## Logistics question/advice
Posts about university-related logistics for university-provided services.

Positive examples:
- How to book a room in Bronfman (3rd floor)
- How do I apply for a major?

Negative examples:
- What’s the difference between level 300 and level 400 political science courses?
- Anyone interested in starting a MTG club at McGill?

Edge cases:
- Where do people print stickers around here?
    - Excluded: The question is about navigating campus/near-campus services, but not necessarily university-related, so it would be a general question.
- Why does the McGill website suck so much at navigation
    - Excluded: Even though it's talking about the McGill website this is more of a complaint than a question or a piece of advice on logistics.

## General question/advice
Posts about general, non course or program related help. It could be university-related or not.

Positive examples:
- A secret route to get from Leacock to McMed/Bellini
- Anyone know how I can print this poster?

Negative examples:
- COMP 520 Compiler Design
- Please don't come to class if you're sick

Edge cases:
- Getting to class with the STM strike
    - Included: This is school-related, but because it's not about a university-provided service, it's just a general question/advice.
- Gym at McGill
    - Excluded: This is a post about the McGill gym which is a university service, so it should be a logistics question.

## Complaint
Posts about someone complaining about something.

Positive examples:
- Some of you guys can't stop vomiting words for 50 mins
- STOP CAUGHING IN CLASSES

Negative examples:
- How to book a room in Bronfman (3rd floor)
- I forgot my minerva password again

Edge cases:
- The COMP310 grading scheme is sooooo bad
    - Included: Even though the post is talking about a specific course, its a complaint and not a question or advice, so it goes in complaints.
- THE MCGILL WEBSITE IS DOWN
    - Excluded: Even though it sounds a little like a complaint, this would be an Event/Announcement as it's informing McGill's students of a situtation.

## Event/Announcement
Posts advertising events or announcing important relavent information for university students. This can also be any type of advertising.

Positive examples:
- Free pizza at the Student Centre this Friday — climate club info session
- Anyone willing to be interviewed for my masters thesis?

Negative examples:
- Favorite graduate-level COMP courses?
- I wanna see a Habs game but these tickets are way too expensive

Edge cases:
- PSA: You can chew with your mouth closed!!!
    - Excluded: Even though is says PSA, it clearly is not giving any actual information, but is a complaint instead.
- Watch out for protestors when leaving your COMP 327 class!
    - Included: Even though its talking about a specific course, the emphasis is on watching out for protestors, which is more of a public service announcement.

## Meme/Funny Post
Posts about something funny or a meme.

Positive examples:
- Me when ChatGPT is down
- This squirrel just stole a hotdog! 

Negative examples:
- Why does everyone think they're the funniest person in the world
- Does anyone have a spare pair of cargo pants they can lend me for my costume?

Edge cases:
- My COMP370 prof saying 67 in class:
    - Included: Even though it's about a specific class, the post is sharing either a meme or a funny video so it goes in this category.
- What elective should I take this winter? I want a course with a funny prof
    - Excluded: Even though its asking about funny profs, the post is more of a program question as it's asking about what course they should take.
